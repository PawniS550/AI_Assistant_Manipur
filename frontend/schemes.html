Agriculture Schemes – Government of Manipur

Major Government Schemes for Farmers

The following schemes are implemented to support farmers financially, technically, and socially.

  • PM‑KISAN
    Income support of ₹6,000 per year to eligible farmer families.
  • Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana (PMFBY)
    Crop insurance coverage against natural calamities and pest attacks.
  • Soil Health Card Scheme
    Soil testing and nutrient recommendations for improved productivity.
  • National Mission on Sustainable Agriculture
    Promotes climate‑resilient agricultural practices.
  • State Agriculture Assistance Programs (Manipur)
    Input subsidies, training, and farmer welfare initiatives.
For official details, contact the Department of Agriculture, Government of Manipur.
